## Gap between the author's absolute numbers and ours

- verdict: **workload mismatch (call count), per-call speed is 1:1** — Reported 54.3 s → 1.9 s *cumulative over a 5K-request ShareGPT run*: `propose()` runs once per decode step per request (~1.2 M calls at ~250 output tokens each), so 54.3 s ≈ 45 µs/call. Ours: `data/pr_13365/bench.py` calls `propose()` **once per sequence** (5000 calls), 0.165 s ≈ 33 µs/call — the same order as the author's per-call cost. The 1.9 s on the author side also includes the one-off Numba JIT (~0.5-1 s) which our bench deliberately warms up outside the timed region, which is why our ratio (63×) exceeds theirs (28×). To match: time the cumulative cost over decode-step-like calls, e.g. for each of the 5000 sequences call `propose(arr[:t], n, k)` for `t` in `range(prompt_len, len(arr))` (≈1.2 M calls), and either include the JIT in the PR's-solution total or report it separately. Hardware is irrelevant (CPU-only, CPU-scaled 1:1).
